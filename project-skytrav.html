A multi-page booking website for SkyTrav, a young travel agency based in Calicut, India, covering holiday packages, group bookings, air tickets, visa assistance and custom tours — with exclusive fares to Dubai, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia and Bahrain.

Challenge

SkyTrav needed a fast, trustworthy web presence for a small four-person team — one that could showcase destination packages, explain services clearly, and make it effortless for a traveller to reach out by call or WhatsApp.

Approach

A focused multi-page structure — Home, Packages, Services, About, Contact — built around destination cards, a persistent booking CTA, and direct call/WhatsApp links so an inquiry is never more than a tap away.

Process

Content and destination package structure first, then page layout, then the booking and contact flows — kept lightweight so the site loads fast on mobile, where most travel searches start.

Result

Live in production at skytrav.in, covering holiday packages, group bookings, air ticketing and visa assistance with direct call and WhatsApp booking flows.
