Questions

Frequently asked.

Do you only work with clients in Kozhikode?

No — while we're based in Kozhikode and Kochi, we work remotely with clients across Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, including Malappuram, Thrissur, Palakkad, Kannur, Coimbatore, Chennai and Mangalore, over video calls, WhatsApp and email.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope and features. As a small freelance studio rather than a large agency, we keep overhead low, so our quotes are typically more affordable than agency pricing. Get in touch for a free, no-obligation quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites take around 4 to 8 weeks from first conversation to launch, depending on scope. See our Process page for how we break that down into five stages.

Do you offer support after the website goes live?

Yes. We stay close to every project after launch to fix issues, make updates and help you measure results.

Can we meet in person, or is everything remote?

Both — we regularly meet clients in person around Kozhikode and Kochi, and work fully remotely with clients further away over video calls.
