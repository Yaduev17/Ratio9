A warm, booking-focused website for Cradle Mom & Baby Care, an Ayurvedic postnatal care centre in Mampuram, Malappuram — covering their care programs, private suites, facilities, doctor consultations and a full stay-booking enquiry flow.

Challenge

Cradle needed a website that felt as calm and trustworthy as the centre itself — reassuring for new and expecting mothers researching a 21-to-42-day stay, while making a detailed booking enquiry effortless.

Approach

A single warm, editorial-toned page — hero, care programs, facilities, testimonials, FAQ and a structured booking form — built around the emotional decision a new mother is making, not just a service listing.

Process

Care programs and stay-duration structure first, then a soft, organic visual language, then a booking form detailed enough (stay length, accommodation type, family members) for the Cradle team to confirm a stay within 24 hours.

Result

Live at yaduev.in/Cradle, covering Ayurvedic postnatal care programs, private AC/non-AC suites, facilities and testimonials, with a structured booking enquiry form plus direct call and WhatsApp fallbacks.
